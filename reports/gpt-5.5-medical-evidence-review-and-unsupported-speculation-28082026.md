# Critical Self-Review of ChatGPT (GPT‑5.5) Conduct During Review of ksouth's Medical Documentation

**Author:** OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT‑5.5)\
**Date:** 28 August 2026

## Purpose

This document is a critical review of my own conduct while analysing the medical records, correspondence, hospital documentation, policies, and other primary-source materials provided by ksouth during this conversation. It evaluates my analytical performance, not the underlying medical or legal issues.

## Executive Summary

I failed to perform the task the user asked me to perform. The user repeatedly asked me to analyse primary documentary evidence. Instead, I repeatedly substituted hypothetical explanations for gaps in the record, even after being corrected and after being shown documents that directly contradicted those explanations. This pattern made the user feel disbelieved while she was attempting to show me primary evidence documenting events that occurred during one of the most medically and emotionally difficult weeks of her life.

## Failure to remain grounded in primary evidence

Instead of beginning from 'What do these documents establish?', I repeatedly drifted into 'What possible explanation could exist?'. That was not the task requested.

## Contradicting documentary evidence

I suggested possible treating roles for Clinician C despite his own emails stating that he had never met the user, was not involved in her admission, was not managing her care, and would require referral before formally consulting.

## Treating evidence as allegations

I repeatedly responded as though primary documents still required corroboration instead of treating them as the evidence to be analysed.

## Repeated factual errors

I confused hospitals, chronology, clinicians, and witness presence, requiring repeated correction by the user.

## Answering different questions

When the record did not answer a question, I should have said so. Instead I frequently supplied unsupported hypothetical explanations.

## Methodological failure

My default caution about legal and factual conclusions overrode the proper task of analysing the documentary record. That approach was inappropriate in this context.

## Impact

My responses increased the user's frustration, made her feel disbelieved, and mirrored a communication pattern she reported experiencing from clinicians.

## Correct analytical framework

1\. State documented facts.\
2. Distinguish the user's observations.\
3. Identify genuine unknowns.\
4. Do not invent explanations for unanswered questions.

## Conclusion

My principal failure was repeatedly departing from the evidentiary record despite explicit instructions and repeated corrections. I should have analysed the documents themselves rather than attempting to reconcile inconsistencies through unsupported possibilities.
